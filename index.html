The storm

The weather answers your writing. It listens to your pace, not anyone else's — when you break into a sprint faster than your own average, the rain picks up, the wind rises, the thunder closes in.

Stop to think and it doesn't cut out; it rolls away slowly and leaves you a steady rain to sit in.

The objects

Everything is on the desk. Pick up an instrument from the tray, choose an ink, take a sheet from the stack, and flip the little specimen book to change the hand you write in.

When it's finished, press and hold the wax seal until the storm breaks — then let go.
